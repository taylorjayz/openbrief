## Description
OpenBrief is a vertical feed of short engineering briefs written in plain language. Every card points at a real free and open-source source (MDN, Wikipedia, project docs, and more) with attribution and license on the card.

Pick topics you care about during onboarding. Those preferences stay on this device so “For you” can prioritize matching briefs. There is no account, no tracker, and no ad profile.

v0.1 beta includes 24 bundled briefs, Home / Feed / Topics tabs, and offline reading. New briefs ship in app updates.

Privacy: Your reading stays on your phone. We don’t collect your data.

## Review notes (for App Review)
OpenBrief shows original short summaries of openly licensed documentation. Each card includes source title, URL, license, and attribution. Content is not a full reprint of third-party articles. The app works offline after install; the only network use is when the tester taps “Open source” to view the original page in the browser. No accounts, purchases, or tracking.
