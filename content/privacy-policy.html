OpenBrief Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

OpenBrief (“the app”) is a vertical feed of short engineering briefs. This policy explains what we do with information when you use the iOS app.

We don’t collect your data

We do not collect personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reports that identify you, or reading history on our servers. There is no account and no sign-in.

On-device preferences only

If you pick interest topics during onboarding, those tags are stored only on your device so the app can sort “For you.” They are not uploaded to us. Deleting the app removes them.

Network use

Briefs ship inside the app. The app does not need a network connection to read them. If you tap a source link (“Open source”), your device opens that public URL in a browser. That visit is between you and the source site, under that site’s own policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If we ever start collecting data, we will update the App Store privacy answers before shipping that version.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on github.com/taylorjayz/openbrief.
