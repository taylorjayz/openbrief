# Privacy marketing lines (OB-007)

Owner: **Sarah** · Product truth: **no data collection** — on-device interest tags only; no analytics SDKs, no accounts, no PII pipeline.

## One-liner (Home / splash)

**Your reading stays on your phone. We don’t collect your data.**

## Short blurb (Home subcopy / Topics footer)

OpenBrief never builds a profile on you. Pick topics you care about — those preferences stay on this device. No tracking SDKs, no account, no “anonymous” event stream to us.

## App Store / Play Store style (short)

OpenBrief is a vertical feed of short engineering briefs from free & open-source sources. Choose topics you like; preferences stay on your device. **We don’t collect your data** — no accounts, no trackers, no ads profile.

## App Store / Play Store style (longer)

OpenBrief helps you learn engineering concepts through short, readable cards sourced from openly licensed docs (MDN, Wikipedia, project docs, and more). Every card shows attribution and license.

Personalization is account-light and private: select interest tags during onboarding; we store them only on your device so “For you” can prioritize matching briefs. **We do not collect analytics, sell data, or sync a profile to our servers.**

## Don’t say

- “Anonymous analytics” / “privacy-first telemetry” (we’re not shipping that)
- “We value your privacy” without the concrete no-collection claim
- Anything that implies CRM, accounts, or cross-device sync

## Do say

- No data collection
- Preferences on-device only
- FOSS sources with visible attribution
