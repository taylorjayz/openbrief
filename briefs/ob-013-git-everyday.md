Most Git work fits a short toolkit: init or clone, status/diff to see reality, add and commit to record intent, switch/branch for topics, merge or rebase to integrate, and pull/push to collaborate. Tags mark known points; restore undoes working-tree mistakes.

Solo developers live in the first half of that list. Participants add clone, fetch/pull, push, and sometimes format-patch. Integrators learn am, revert, and careful publishing.

Start fluent with status, diff, log, and commit—then grow into remotes. Twenty commands beat memorizing the entire manual on day one.
