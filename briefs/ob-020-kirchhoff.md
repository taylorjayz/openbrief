Kirchhoff’s current law says the sum of currents into a node equals the sum leaving it—charge doesn’t pile up at a junction. Kirchhoff’s voltage law says the directed sum of voltages around a closed loop is zero—energy conservation in the circuit.

Together with Ohm’s law, they let you analyze resistor networks systematically: write equations, solve for unknowns, check that results make physical sense (no negative resistances from algebra mistakes).

Bench habit: if measurements disagree with KCL/KVL, suspect a wiring error or a meter in the wrong mode before blaming the theory.
